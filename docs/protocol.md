# Tennis elbow rehab protocol (source for the app's content)

Source: a video transcript excerpt (1:12:54 to 2:38:43). The video is from a coaching business, so treat the numeric rules as practical guidance, not trial-derived thresholds. Earlier chapters of the video were not available. Not a diagnosis.

## Movement screen (rate pain 0-10 per test, rank, build the plan around the top 3)
| Key | Test | How |
|---|---|---|
| ecrl | Extensor carpi radialis longus | Arm straight, grip handle, push it away from you (radial deviation) while other hand resists. Repeat with elbow bent: less pain confirms ECRL. Pain is just above the bony point. |
| ecrb | Extensor carpi radialis brevis | Wrist cocked back ("motorcycle"), other hand twists handle away. |
| ecu | Extensor carpi ulnaris | Arm straight, pull handle toward you (ulnar deviation) against resistance. |
| sup | Supinator | Elbow 90 degrees, turn forearm outward (palm up, "bowl of soup") against resistance. |
| ed | Extensor digitorum (finger extensors) | Arm straight, fingers extended, push each finger down (2 to 5); score the most painful finger. |
| nerve | Radial nerve (optional) | Fist with thumb across, arm at side, flex wrist fully, internally rotate shoulder, press shoulder down with other hand, abduct arm to shoulder level. Shooting pain in radial pattern is positive. Treatment is a nerve glide: head tilts toward then away from the shoulder. |
Rule: nerve only counts toward priorities if scored 3 or more. Ties broken by test order above. Also assess neck and shoulder compensations in a full assessment (not covered in the excerpt).

## Exercises (hold dumbbells at the far end for leverage)
- ecrl: hammer curl with radial deviation (curl to shoulder while tilting wrist to thumb side).
- ecrb: wrist extension with forearm on a foam roller (roller just above elbow), lower and lift.
- ecu: ulnar deviation, dumbbell held at the top end, arm at side, bend wrist toward little finger.
- sup: supination/pronation with dumbbell held at the bottom end, arm straight on foam roller.
- ed: finger extension against a loop band, arm straight on roller.
- nerve: radial nerve glide.
Equipment: weights beat bands or flex bars for long-term results; bands are fine for travel. Anything is better than nothing.

## Phases (a road map, not strict; drop back after a flare)
| Phase | Style | Dose | Frequency | Intensity / volume |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reduce | Isometric holds (30-45 s) or light pump work | Isometrics 3-5 x 30-45 s; pump 2-3 x ~30 reps | Daily, 5-7 days/week | Low / high. Goal: bring pain from ~6 to ~3/10 |
| Rebuild | Isotonic, full range, 3 s down / 3 s up | 2-5 sets x 15-20 reps | Daily, or alternate days if recovering poorly | Minimal-moderate / medium-high |
| Return | Heavier isotonic | 2-3 sets x 10-15 reps | Alternate days | Moderate / moderate |
| Reinforce | Plyometrics or heavy slow resistance | Plyo 2-3 x 8-15; HSR 3 s down / 3 s up | 2-3 days/week | Moderate-high / low-moderate |
3 to 10 exercises per session, 15 to 25 minutes a day. Eccentric-only work is not preferred over normal full-range lifts.

## Rules
- Pain target: 3/10 or less during rehab and for 48 hours after (delayed tendon response).
- Effort about 7/10 (could do 2 more reps, not 4), but if pain is high, lower the load first. Frequency stays high early.
- Tempo 3 s down / 3 s up, controlled load (avoids stress shielding of the injured fibres).
- Early: low intensity, high volume, high frequency. Later: higher intensity, lower volume, lower frequency.
- Flare-up: halve load and time until pain is 3/10 or less for 48 h; higher volume, lower intensity; heat, massage; keep moving. About 80% of people flare at least once; shorter flares are a progress sign.
- Return to sport/gym order: duration first, then frequency, then intensity. Start ~30 min, once a week, ~3/10 intensity; at most 1 hour; no back-to-back days early. Apply the 48-hour rule.
- Progress signs: more confidence with mug/door, less morning pain, burning to sharp to dull ache to sore to none, same pain with more activity, shorter flares.
- Timeline: mild 6-10 weeks, moderate 10-16, chronic 3-6+ months. Pain graph zig-zags; judge by the average.
- Adjuncts (claims from the video, modest evidence): sleep with elbow on a pillow at 90 degrees; collagen ~20 g/day with vitamin C; enough protein; omega-3; massage, pin-and-stretch, scraping, heat.
- Consistency over intensity: track inputs (did the rehab), not just outputs (pain).

## Measures
- Daily 0-10 pain (morning, during rehab, worst, evening), pain character, task pain (bottle cap, mug grip, door handle), sleep hours/quality/woke with pain.
- Weekly PRTEE (15 items: 5 pain items summed 0-50, 10 function items summed then halved 0-50, total 0-100, lower better; about 11 points often cited as meaningful change) and optional pain-free grip strength.
- Gym impact: compare next-morning pain and peak pain over the next 48 h after training days vs rest days, split by how much the session loaded the elbow.
